Electronic Products Shipping Guidelines

1. Order Processing & Dispatch

All verified electronic orders are prepared, bench-tested where applicable, and handed over to our courier partners within 24 to 48 business hours of confirmation.

Orders placed on weekends or national holidays will be dispatched on the following business day. Once your order leaves our facility, you will receive an automated dispatch confirmation via email and SMS containing your carrier details and tracking number.

2. Shipping Timelines & Coverage

We provide nationwide express delivery across major metro cities, urban hubs, and regional zones through trusted logistics partners including BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, and FedEx.

Estimated Delivery Timelines:

  • Metro Cities & Tier-1 Hubs: 2 to 4 business days.
  • Tier-2 and Regional Cities: 3 to 6 business days.
  • Special Delivery / Remote Locations: 5 to 8 business days.
3. Secure Electronic Packaging

We recognize that premium electronics, audio drivers, displays, and precision components require exceptional care in transit. Every product is packaged using:

  • Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) protective sleeves for sensitive electronics.
  • Multi-layer heavy-gauge shock absorbing bubble protection.
  • Reinforced multi-wall corrugated outer cartons.
  • Tamper-evident branded security tape on all seams.

Do not accept any package if the security tape is cut, tampered with, or visibly compromised upon delivery.

4. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery

Free Standard Shipping: Applicable on all prepaid and Cash-on-Delivery (COD) orders exceeding ₹499 (or store currency equivalent).

For orders below ₹499, a nominal flat shipping fee of ₹49 is added at checkout to cover regional logistics handling.

5. Live Shipment Tracking

You can monitor your shipment every step of the way. Visit our Order Tracking Page and enter your Order ID or Courier Waybill/AWB number to view real-time location checkpoints.

6. Damaged or Lost Shipments

All shipments are fully insured against loss or transit damage. If an item arrives physically damaged, please report it within 48 hours of delivery with unboxing photographs or video to marketing@wisetrack.in or through our Returns Portal. We will arrange a prompt priority replacement at zero additional charge.
